Welcome 👋

Get the Unity plugin running in four steps. It talks straight to a free AI provider from inside the Editor, so there's nothing to host and no paid Claude key.

1

Add the plugin to your Unity project

Copy the unity/UnityGUI folder from the repo into your project's Assets/ folder. It's Editor-only code, so it never ships in your build.

Open the repo → View the plugin folder
2

Get a free AI key

Pick a free provider and grab its key: Google Gemini (free, no credit card) or Groq (free), or use Ollama locally with no key. Paste it into the plugin window — it stays on your machine.

Free Gemini key → Free Groq key Ollama (no key)
3

Open the generator in Unity

In Unity's top menu, open:

Window ▸ UnityGUI ▸ AI Game Generator

Pick your provider & model, paste your free key, and describe your game.

4

Generate & press Play

Hit Generate. The plugin writes ready-to-run C# into Assets/UnityGUI/Generated/. Open an empty scene and press Play — the generated code bootstraps the whole game itself.
